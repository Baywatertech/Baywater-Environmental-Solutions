## 📌 About Us

**Baywater** is a specialized environmental engineering firm headquartered in Casablanca, Morocco. We engineer, deploy, and scale advanced, eco-efficient treatment and climate technologies. Our core expertise spans containerized mobile units, complex industrial effluent remediation, sludge management, and zero-liquid-discharge systems.

### Core Capabilities
* **Landfill Leachate Remediation:** High-capacity mobile containerized treatment units.
* **Wastewater Treatment:** Advanced industrial & municipal effluent purification.
* **Sludge Management:** Dewatering, stabilization, and volumetric reduction.
* **Seawater Desalination:** Modular, containerized reverse-osmosis & ionic systems.
* **Air & Surface Sterilization:** Indoor & outdoor pathogen and contaminant neutralization.
* **Atmospheric Water Generation:** Turning ambient humidity into potable water.
* **Climate & Humidity Control:** Industrial-scale thermal and moisture regulation.
